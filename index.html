🧠 How the Integrated Engine Works

60% Quantitative
Momentum, trend, relative strength, volume, RSI quality, breakout, volatility and risk/reward.
20% Company Context
Company-specific news and its direction/impact.
10% Industry Context
Sector and industry-level external conditions.
10% Market Context
Broader macro, commodity, government and geopolitical environment.
Important scoring distinction
Company / Industry / Market values shown on stock cards are context inputs. The separate Context value is the weighted contribution used by the integrated engine.
Conflict rule
Strong technical setups facing materially negative external context are flagged rather than automatically rejected.
Decision layer
The app derives a research posture from quantitative strength, context balance, confidence and conflict flags. It is an interpretation layer over the supplied data, not a guaranteed prediction or automatic buy/sell signal.
Important: Indian Stock Pro provides quantitative research signals and contextual analysis. It does not guarantee future returns and does not issue automatic buy or sell decisions. Verify current prices and market conditions before acting.
