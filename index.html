🧠 How Indian Stock Pro Works

1. Quantitative Screening
Momentum, trend, relative strength, volume, RSI quality, breakout potential, volatility and risk/reward are evaluated.
2. Company News
Company-specific news receives higher contextual importance because it can directly affect the stock.
3. Industry Context
The system checks whether the broader industry environment supports or opposes the quantitative setup.
4. Market Context
Macro, commodity, geopolitical and broad-market conditions are considered.
5. Conflict Detection
If quantitative momentum and external context materially disagree, the system identifies a conflict and increases contextual risk.
6. Integrated Score
The final ranking combines quantitative evidence with contextual evidence while keeping quantitative analysis as the dominant component.
Important: Indian Stock Pro provides quantitative and contextual research signals. It does not guarantee future returns and is not investment advice. Always verify current market prices, company news, fundamentals and other relevant information before making an investment decision.
